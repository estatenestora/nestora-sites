Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

1. Overview

Nestora ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and related web platforms.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, place a booking, or interact with Nestora, we collect:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, mobile phone number, and voluntary profile information.
  • Location Information: Precise or approximate location data (only with your explicit permission) to match you with nearby service providers and facilitate on-site visits.
  • Transaction & Payment Information: Payment gateway order references, transaction identifiers, and amounts. Note: We do not collect or store full credit/debit card numbers or bank account passwords; all payment operations are handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant aggregators (e.g., PhonePe).
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, push notification tokens, and application activity logs to ensure optimal performance and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process service bookings and dispatch verified service providers.
  • To verify account identity and prevent fraudulent activities.
  • To provide automated booking status updates, notifications, and customer support.
  • To process refunds and resolve transaction queries.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for advertising or commercial marketing.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We share relevant customer information strictly on a need-to-know basis with:

  • Assigned Service Providers: Name, contact number, and service delivery address to enable task completion.
  • Payment Aggregators & Banking Partners: Authorized gateway partners (e.g., PhonePe) to process authorized booking fees and wallet top-ups.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by Indian law, regulatory mandate, or court order.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical and administrative security measures, including end-to-end encryption for sensitive transmissions and role-based access control. Your personal information is retained only as long as your account remains active or as required by applicable statutory obligations.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To request account deactivation and data deletion, please email us at estatenestora@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Requests are processed within 30 days.
